Free PDF to Excel Converter

PDF2Excel is a free online PDF to Excel converter that helps you convert supported text-based PDF documents into Excel XLSX spreadsheets. The tool is designed to make PDF conversion simple and convenient.

How to Convert PDF to Excel

  1. Click Browse File and select your PDF.
  2. Click Convert to Excel.
  3. Wait for the conversion to finish.
  4. Click Download Excel to save your XLSX file.
