About PDF2Excel

Welcome to PDF2Excel, a free online tool designed to help users convert text-based PDF documents into Excel spreadsheets quickly and easily.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to provide a simple and convenient way to extract useful information from PDF documents and organize it into Excel files.

How PDF2Excel Works

PDF2Excel uses browser-based technology to process supported PDF documents. Users can select a PDF file, convert it, and download the resulting Excel file.

Privacy

We value your privacy. Our converter is designed to process supported files directly in your browser, without requiring users to create an account.

If you have questions, suggestions, or feedback about PDF2Excel, please contact us through our Contact page.
